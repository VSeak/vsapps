# PT Pal

The owner's PT motivator: after each physical therapy set, tap a berry to feed their pal. Red **Sweetberry** = daily PT (counts toward the flame and a daily goal), gold **Cragberry** = climb day PT, which also counts toward the daily goal (names the user picked; the code and database still say `daily` and `climb`) (the owner has no set climbing days, so they pick which berry to throw). One user: only the owner's login gets in (`pt_owner`).

## How it's built

- Same shape as Kiddo Draw: `index.html` (header and shell), `styles.css`, plain JS in `js/` loaded as classic `<script>` tags that share one global scope; `boot()` runs last. No build step. supabase-js pinned from jsDelivr.
  - `core.js`, `app.js`: copied from Kiddo Draw (helpers, sign-in pages, `loadMe`, `route`, install banner). Routes: `#/` Feed, `#/history`, `#/settings`.
  - `pal.js`: `loadSettings`, `photoUrl` (downloads the private photo, kept as an object URL until `photo_at` changes), `loadFeeds` (all rows, 1000 at a time), day, streak, and flame math (`byDay`, `streakOf`, `bestStreak`, `flameOf`, in the phone's time zone), and the drawings (`berrySvg`, `flameSvg`, `foxSvg`).
  - `room.js`: the cozy room behind Feed (the user asked): wall, baseboard, floorboards, a couch cut off at the left, a plant on the right, a picture on the wall at the right with the window on the left (the user swapped them), and a rug under the pal. The fireplace on the back wall is the flame meter (the user's idea; it replaced the flame chip): `setFire` puts `lvl-N` on `#fire`, and styles.css draws each step (just the logs at 0, then the yellow flame, red, bigger, side flames at 6 and 7) with a glow on the wall and floor that grows with it, a flare (`whoosh`) when it steps up while you watch, and sparks out of it after a week at full size. `placeRoom` keeps `--floor-y` at the fireplace's foot (ResizeObserver), so the floor line follows the layout. The pal stands on the rug just below the fire and keeps its full size (the user asked); on short phones (max-height 760px) the fireplace shrinks and the spacing tightens instead so the berries fit without scrolling. Dark mode is the same room at night (room colors are tokens; the glow is stronger, `--glow-k`).
    More cozy (the user asked): the couch is drawn far past its left edge so it runs off the screen (not a rounded end), with a plaid throw blanket draped over its backrest behind the pillow (the user moved it off the arm); a side table in front of its end holds a mug with three wisps of steam. The **window** (`setSky`) shows the real sky: `sunTimes` works out sunrise and sunset from `pt_settings.lat/lon` once a day (standard solar formulas; checked against New York, London, and Sydney within a few minutes); dawn and dusk are the half hour either side; no location or polar day/night → 7am and 7pm. Night has a moon in its real phase (`moonPhase`) and twinkling stars; day a drifting cloud; snow falls in Dec–Feb (Jun–Aug south of the equator). The half-second timer re-checks every 30 s. The pal breathes (`.pal-breathe`, a 1.5% rise from the feet, paused during the bounce) and gets a rim light: the `#rimLight` SVG filter (feed.js) brightens only a thin band inside the outline, thicker on top, with strength set by `setFire` (`#rimFlood`); the old outer halo lit the whole pal (the user saw it). The firelight on the wall and floor (`.glow-wall`, `.glow-floor`) lives in `.room` behind the furniture, placed by `--fire-x`/`--fire-gy` and strengthened by `data-lvl` on `.feed` (it used to cover the couch and plant). Dust specks (`speck`) float in front (`#dust`, z 2; they used to rise from behind the fireplace) near the fireplace from step 3, at most step − 1 on screen (not too dusty), each wandering through three random waypoints with a slight upward pull. Settings → Window: Use My Location (asked once, saved rounded to 0.1°), Update, or Forget.
  - `holiday.js` (loaded before `room.js`, whose drawings use it): **Christmas** (the user asked), from the day after Thanksgiving (4th Thursday of November) through January 7 (`xmasNow`; `.feed.xmas` shows every `.xmas-only` part and hides the plant): a garland in swags with 12 twinkling lights (glow grows with the fire via `--rim`) over two stockings (toes out) on the mantel, a wreath on the window (`.win-wreath`), marshmallows and a candy cane in the mug (the cane is drawn before the mug so it sits inside it, and tipped 30° (the user picked it) so it leans toward the right lip with the hook hanging outside, slid 3 units out of the cup; the user saw it stuck to the side, then standing straight up), and the plant is put away for a big **advent tree** in its corner, with the picture still on the wall above it (the user found the plant-sized one far too small, then asked for the picture back once the tree sat lower; tree part about 110px wide on a phone, 150px wide screens). Each December 1–24 day that meets the goal hangs one ornament (`ornamentDays`, max 24; today counts once met): deep red, cream, plum, and every fourth a Sweetberry; gold with a twinkling sparkle on a stuffed day. Fixed spots (`ORN_SPOTS`) filled in a spread-out order (`ORN_ORDER`). Each ornament also adds a present (`GIFT_SPOTS`: a heap growing from under the tree out left and down: big boxes at the back, rows of smaller ones in front staggered like bricks and covering their bottoms, plus three small ones stacked on top (the user saw loose boxes, then all one size); ribbon and bow scale with the box. The biggest four stand behind the tree (`back` = 1, drawn into `#giftsBack` before it); in front, nothing over the tree reaches above its lowest branches, so no ornament is covered (the user saw them hidden). The tree stands a little forward on the floor (the user asked it lower)); presents are gone from December 26, the tree stays to January 7. A new ornament or present (or an ornament turning gold) pops in (scale up past full, then settle) with a small sparkle burst (`pop`); the page's first draw doesn't pop (`shownOrn = null`). The **Santa hat** shows on the pal in the same weeks: `settings.hat` `{x, y, w, r}` = the middle of its fur band as fractions of the photo, its width, its tilt; null = none on a photo, `FOX_HAT` on the drawing. `palPic` (pal.js) wraps the pal in `.pal-body`, a box the photo's own shape (`--ar` from the image's onload) standing bottom middle, so fractions work at any size; the bounce is on `.pal-body` so the hat bounces too. Settings → Santa Hat (the card shows only in the Christmas weeks; the user asked) → Place Hat: drag it (or tap where it goes, or arrow keys), Size and Tilt sliders. A new or removed photo sets `hat` back to null. All the decor is worked out from the berries; nothing is saved but the hat.
    **Thanksgiving** (the user asked), November 1 through Thanksgiving Day (`fallNow`; `.feed.fall` shows `.fall-only` and hides the plant; `.feed.tday` shows `.tday-only` on the day itself); Christmas starts the next day. Brown leaves blow tumbling across the window (`.leafy`, set in `setSky`); the mug holds cider with a cinnamon stick (no orange slice; the user asked); the plant's corner gets a long **harvest table** (`harvestTableSvg`, a cloth with a rust stripe, running off the right edge) with a wicker cornucopia at its right end. The user passed on a mantel garland and a window wreath, and on pumpkins by the plant. Each November day up to Thanksgiving that meets the goal sets one piece of food on it (`harvestDays`, up to 28): apple, mini pumpkin, pear, grapes, corn, and a Sweetberry in turn, spilling out of the horn and heaping left (`HARVEST_SPOTS`: rows sitting in the dips of the row below, filled so nothing floats; the heap clears the fireplace on a 375px phone). A stuffed day's piece is the same food in gold with a sparkle (the user asked: not a gold pumpkin). New pieces pop in, and so does one turning gold; one losing its gold (an Undo) doesn't pop (ornaments now work the same). On Thanksgiving Day a slice of pumpkin pie sits on the side table and a stuffed pal "is stuffed like a turkey!". The pal wears an **autumn beanie** (`beanieSvg`, drawn so its cuff's middle is where the Santa hat's fur band is) using the same `settings.hat` placement, so no SQL: `hatSeason()` picks the hat, and Settings shows "Autumn Beanie" in November and "Santa Hat" at Christmas. Year in Berries adds "Thanksgiving table: N of M days."
  - `feed.js`: the Feed page. Daily berry bottom left, climb berry bottom right (the user asked). One Cragberry a day (the user asked): after one, it greys out with "Done for today" until Undo or midnight (the half-second timer notices midnight even with reduced motion on). History follows the same rule: a day's Add Cragberry turns into "Cragberry Added" (off) once it has one, and comes back if it's deleted. Only the page enforces it, not the database. A tap saves a row right away (id made on the phone so Undo works at once), then `toss` throws the berry from its button into the pal: across linearly, up and over in an arc, and shrinking the whole way (the user asked: "starts close and large-ish… smaller and smaller until it disappears"). Three nested elements so each motion has its own easing (Web Animations API). Then `celebrate`: the pal bounces and six shake marks pop out around it and jiggle (the user asked for excitement). Undo for 5 seconds between the two berries (the user asked: under the header it covered the flame). The line under the name: "would love a berry", "would love N more berries", "is full and happy" (goal met), "is stuffed!" (passed).
    **Touching the pal** (the user's partner asked; `palTouch`, `#pal` is a button, Enter/Space = a tap): **tap = boop**, a squish plus a reaction to today: no berries → a speech bubble with a Sweetberry and "?", partway → "More berries please!", goal met → six hearts pop out in a fan (no bubble), stuffed → "So full…" with sparkles (and 🦃 on Thanksgiving Day). **Press and hold (400 ms) = pet**: the pal rocks side to side and a heart floats up every 450 ms until you let go (no eyes closing: it's a photo). **Five taps in 2 seconds = dizzy**: a hop, one full spin, then a wobble while three gold stars and two swirls circle its head (at the hat's `y` if placed, else near the top), about 3 seconds. Taps are ignored while a berry is flying in and the pal bounces (`palBusy`, 1.5 s) and while dizzy; a berry tap stops petting. `.happy` and `.booped` come off when they finish, or the bounce would replay when a pet or dizzy (later in the CSS, same specificity) ends. Reduced motion: no squish, rock, or spin; the bubble and hearts fade in place, and the stars sit still.
    Effects (the user asked), one half-second timer (`startFx`) that only runs on Feed with the app on screen: **goal passed** → the passing berry sets off a burst of sparkles, then one or two twinkle around the pal at an uneven ~1.5 s beat for the rest of the day (behind everything, stops at midnight); **a week at full fire** → sparks float up out of the fireplace (`ember`). Reduced motion skips the throw, bounce, marks, sparkles, and sparks.
  - `history.js`: stats (current and best streak, full days, stuffed days (the user asked: a 6th card so phones show 3 + 3, not 3 + 2 with a gap; the four monthly cards say "This Month" or the month shown, like "Oct 2026", on a small second line; the streaks are all time), Sweetberry and Cragberry counts this month), a month calendar (filled = goal met, gold sparkle = stuffed, Sweetberry icons up to 3 then +n (the user asked), gold berry = Cragberry; a legend sits under it instead of a paragraph: the Sweetberry and Cragberry icons and the stuffed sparkle, on one line. The user dropped "Goal Met" from it and found "tap a day" obvious), a day dialog to delete a berry or add a forgotten one (noon that day, or now for today), and a month and year picker from the month name (the user asked; future months off, a dot on months with berries).
    **Year in Berries** (the user asked): a card under the calendar, **January only** and **only years already over** (‹ ›, the first berry's year to last year; it starts on the year just ended): berries, Sweetberries, Cragberries, full days, stuffed days, best streak inside that year, and the year's Christmas tree ("13 of 24 ornaments"). `newYear()` shows last year's as a "Happy New Year!" dialog with sparkles the first time the app is opened in a new year (whatever the date; from Feed), once on any device: `year_seen` is saved before it shows. Skipped if last year had no berries.
  - `settings.js`: the photo (upload, replace, remove), the season's hat (Santa hat or autumn beanie), the pal's name, and the daily goal.
- **The flame** (the user designed it; shown as the fireplace, no number): 7 steps. 0 just the logs (start, and the floor; the user dropped the ash and coals: they popped in and out), 1 a yellow flame, 2 red joins at the same size (going 2 → 1 the red shrinks down behind the yellow, half its size, then goes; 1 → 2 it grows back out; the user asked: fires shrink, they don't fade), 3–7 bigger each step; side flames lean out in a deeper orange. Going down eases without the bounce (a bouncy shrink overshot below zero and flipped the flame); going out, the flame shrinks into the logs and is hidden only at the end (no fade). The left side flame is mirrored (`--f: -1`). Custom properties `--s`, `--sl`, `--sr`, and `--g` are registered numbers (`@property`): never reuse those names for anything else (sparkles once used `--s` for a px size and went 0 wide; they use `--sz`). Each finished day: goal met → up one (the goal counts Sweetberries and the day's Cragberry, `berriesOn`; the user asked); some berries short of it → holds; none → down one. Passing the goal still only goes up one. Today counts only once its goal is met. Worked out from every row since the first berry, with today's goal, so changing the goal changes the past too (the user was told; saving each day's goal would need SQL).
- **Streak** (History stats only): days in a row with at least one Sweetberry; today without one yet doesn't break it. Cragberries never count against anything.
- **Stuffed** (`isStuffed` in pal.js; the goal-passed sparkles, "is stuffed!", and History's sparkle): going past the daily goal, with the Cragberry counting toward it (`berriesOn(e) > goal`), so the goal plus a Cragberry is stuffed. Whichever berry completes it sets off the burst.
- **One photo at a time** (the user asked): it's always saved as `pal` in the private `pt-photo` bucket with `upsert`, so a new upload overwrites the old file. The phone shrinks it to about 600px (WebP, or PNG where WebP can't be made) first. No photo = the fox kit drawing. The user wanted their pal to look like a Pokémon; Claude doesn't draw that, so the app has a photo slot instead and the drawing is original.
- **Same Supabase project as the other apps.** Tables are all `pt_*`; `supabase/schema.sql` creates them and the bucket. Every rule is `pt_is_owner()`: the signed-in email is in `pt_owner` (one row, set in the SQL Editor; the same email as `fire_owner`). Any other login sees "No Access".
- `login_in_other_app()` is replaced here to answer for `team_staff`, `fire_owner`, `xmas_owner`, and `pt_owner`, so this schema's version must be the last one run. If another app's schema is ever re-run, re-run that function from this schema after it.
- Errors go to the shared `app_errors` with `app = 'pt'` (the schema widens its check).
- The root home page hides its card unless `localStorage` `vsapps.ptOwner` is set (`loadMe` sets or clears it, Sign Out clears it); tapping Welcome 5 times shows it.
- The photo is in Supabase Storage, not Postgres, so the nightly database backup doesn't hold it. The berries and settings are backed up.

## Data

- `pt_settings(id, name, daily_goal, photo_at, lat, lon, hat, year_seen)`: one row. `hat` jsonb `{x, y, w, r}` (the Santa hat on the photo; null = not placed), `year_seen` the year whose New Year screen was shown (`migrations/2026-11-13-hat-and-new-year.sql`). `daily_goal` 1–10. `photo_at` null = no photo. `lat`/`lon`: the owner's location rounded to 0.1° (about 10 km) for the window's real sunrise and sunset, null = not set (`migrations/2026-11-13-window-location.sql`). The page selects `*`, so it still loads before a new column's SQL is run.
- `pt_feeds(id, kind, fed_at)`: one row per berry, `kind` daily or climb.
- Storage bucket `pt-photo` (private, 2 MB, PNG/JPG/WebP): one file, `pal`. Policies on `storage.objects` let only the owner read, add, change, or delete it, and only under that name.

## Conventions

- Look: "Berry & Ember": a cozy room on Feed (above), peach-cream page elsewhere, deep plum ink, berry red accent, gold, and leaf green. Fredoka headings, Nunito body. Tokens on `:root` with dark mode. Logo: a red berry (`icon.svg`; PNG icons drawn with headless Edge, 180px shrunk with System.Drawing).
- Works at ~400px wide. Title case for buttons, headings, and labels; sentence case for hints and messages.
